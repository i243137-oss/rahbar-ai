# Academic Rules Handbook (Old 2022 Edition)

## 1. Legacy Course Withdrawal Rules
Under the legacy 2022 regulations, course withdrawal was permitted strictly up to the eighth week of classes.
This edition is obsolete and replaced by subsequent handbooks.

## 2. Legacy Probation Guidelines
A student with a CGPA below 1.70 was subject to immediate academic dismissal.
