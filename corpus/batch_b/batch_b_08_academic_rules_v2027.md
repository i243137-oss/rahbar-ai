# Academic Rules Handbook (2027 Edition)

## 1. Academic Assessment and Warning
Effective Spring 2027, the course withdrawal deadline has been revised to the twelfth week of the regular semester.
Students on academic warning must register for a maximum of 12 credit hours per semester until their cumulative GPA rises above 2.00.

## 2. Degree Requirements
All undergraduate students must complete 130 to 134 credit hours and achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.00 to qualify for the award of a degree.
