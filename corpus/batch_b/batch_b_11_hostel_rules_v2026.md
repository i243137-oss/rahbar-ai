# Campus Student Hostel Regulations

## 1. Hostel Admissions and Eligibility
Hostel accommodation is allocated each academic year based on distance from home city and semester GPA.
The deadline to apply for Fall semester hostel residency is August 15.

## 2. Curfew and Gate Timings
All resident students must return to their respective hostel premises by 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.
Nightly curfew is strictly enforced by the resident warden and biometric gate turnstiles.

## 3. Disciplinary Actions
Possession of unauthorized electrical appliances, heaters, or cooking stoves inside resident dorm rooms incurs a mandatory fine of Rs. 5,000.

## 4. Room Allocation and Transfers
A resident student seeking a room change or transfer must submit a formal written application to the Resident Warden within the first two weeks of the semester. Room changes are subject to space availability and warden approval.
