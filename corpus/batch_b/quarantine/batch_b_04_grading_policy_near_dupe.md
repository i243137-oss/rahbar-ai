# FAST-NUCES Grading Policy Handbook

## 1. Letter Grades and Grade Points
The university awards letter grades ranging from A+ (4.00) down to F (0.00).
A student must achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.00 in undergraduate degree programs to graduate in good academic standing.

## 2. Incomplete Course Policy
An incomplete grade (I) is awarded when a candidate completes all course requirements except the final examination due to genuine medical emergency.
The incomplete grade must be resolved before the end of the next regular semester.

## 3. Attendance Regulations
A student must maintain at least 80 percent attendance in each registered subject to be eligible to sit for the final examination.
